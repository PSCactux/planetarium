Data notices for the files in this folder
=========================================

stars.json
  Derived from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Edition
  (Hoffleit, D. & Warren, W. H. Jr., 1991), distributed by CDS as catalogue V/50
  (https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/V/50). Public domain (NASA ADC).
  Proper names follow the IAU Working Group on Star Names (WGSN) list; the
  name-to-star mapping was written by hand for this project.

constellations.json
  Constellation lines and label positions from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn
  (https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), used under the BSD 3-Clause license
  reproduced below. The lines follow the IAU constellation charts. Only the
  coordinates, IDs, Latin names and ranks were kept; coordinates were converted
  from GeoJSON longitude to right ascension.
